# Victoria: the within-SA4 tests with vacant-land prices

Generated by `scripts/analyse_land_value.py` from committed files. Every figure is computed; do not edit by hand. The Phase 2 tests are in `LOCATION_FACTOR.md`; this re-runs them for Victoria with land cost measured directly. Region-level and ecological throughout.

## Why

Phase 2 measured cost by the 2021 Census median mortgage repayment. That is high where housing is new, because recent buyers carry larger loans, so growth areas read as dear. Valuer-General Victoria publishes the median sale price of vacant residential land by locality, which is land cost itself: the median sale of a vacant residential home site or surveyed lot under 4,000 m², by calendar year.

## The land measure

Medians VGV marks `*` repeat the previous year's for want of any sales and are not used; `^` marks a year with fewer than 10 sales and is used here, then dropped under Sensitivity (definitions from VGV's *A Guide to Property Values 2025*, p. 10).

Each locality's mean log median, 2021–2023 (VGV's 2015–2025 time series), carried to SA3 by the locality's 2021 dwellings in each SA3 (ABS Mesh Blocks). An SA3 is used when localities with a median hold at least 25% of its dwellings: 41 of 66 Victorian SA3s, holding 80% of Victoria's 2,092 new-build SDA dwellings. The rest are almost all inner and middle Melbourne, which is built out and sells little vacant land: Banyule, Bayside, Boroondara, Brunswick - Coburg, Darebin - North, Darebin - South, Essendon, Glen Eira, Hobsons Bay, Keilor, Kingston, Knox, Loddon - Elmore, Manningham - East, Maribyrnong, Melbourne City, Monash, Moreland - North, Nillumbik - Kinglake, Port Phillip, Stonnington - East, Stonnington - West, Whitehorse - East, Whitehorse - West, Yarra.

Within SA4s, relative land price and relative mortgage agree in rank (+0.66), but land spreads much wider: a within-SA4 standard deviation of 0.19 log points against 0.08. Growth corridors (5 of these SA3s) sit -0.04 log points from their SA4's mean on land price and +0.02 on mortgage.

## What it can and cannot support

- **Does general building lean dear on land price?** All approvals here lean +0.35 with the Census mortgage (toward dearer SA3s) but +0.02 with vacant-land price (about neutral). So the apparent lean of general building toward dear SA3s comes from the mortgage measure, which reads new housing as dear, not from land.
- **Does SDA lean cheap on land price?** SDA share against relative land price: -0.31 (p 0.156). Net of approvals, need, population and area, the regression gives -19.13 (± 8.08) share points per log point and the multinomial -1.28 (± 0.50); the rank difference from approvals is -0.18 (p 0.425).
- **The placebo, on land.** Against population, new build leans -0.48 (p 0.023) with land price; existing pre-NDIS stock -0.09 (p 0.673). On the Census mortgage the two looked alike; on land they do not: pre-NDIS stock shows no lean toward cheap land, new SDA does.
- **Small sample.** 12 Victorian SA4s qualify; inner Melbourne is missing because it sells no vacant land, so these are comparisons among fringe, middle-ring and regional SA3s only.
- **Per lot, not per square metre.** VGV's vacant-land class is home sites under 4,000 m², which excludes lifestyle and acreage blocks, but lots within it still vary (a 2,000–3,999 m² regional site against a 400 m² estate lot), so a median lot price is not a price per square metre. VGV does not state the workbook's class in words; its quarterly report titles the same table "median vacant residential land prices".
- **Victoria and NSW only.** NSW, with Valuer General land values, is in `LAND_VALUE_NSW.md`; Queensland, South Australia and Western Australia publish no comparable free series.
