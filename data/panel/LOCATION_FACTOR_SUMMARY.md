# Location-factor experiment: executive summary

Generated by `scripts/summarise_location_factor.py` from the JSON behind the three reports; every figure is read from them, so do not edit by hand. Start here, then read the reports listed under *Where things are*. All findings are ecological: they compare regions' published totals and follow no dwelling, provider or participant.

## The question

The NDIS pays an SDA provider a base amount (set by building type, design category and features) times a **location factor** set per SA4 and building type. The factor is the same across every SA3 in an SA4, but land costs are not, so the margin is highest on the SA4's cheapest land. **Hypothesis:** providers therefore build new SDA in the cheapest SA3s within each SA4, more than ordinary housing does (a market developer's sale price rises with land value; SDA's payment does not).

## The answer so far

1. **New SDA is built where new housing is built.** The growth-corridor SA3s hold 29% of new SDA (Jun 2023–Jun 2026) against 28% of dwelling approvals, 12% of population, 13% of participants waiting for SDA and 7% of older SDA stock. *Solid, national.*
2. **Within an SA4, new SDA leans toward the cheaper land, as the hypothesis predicts.** In Victoria, with Valuer-General vacant-land prices (2021–2023): general building is neutral (+0.02), new SDA leans cheaper net of approvals, need, population and area (regression -19.13 (± 8.08); multinomial -1.28 (± 0.50)), and pre-NDIS SDA stock does not lean (-0.09, p 0.673; new SDA -0.48, p 0.023). *Suggestive: Victoria only, 12 SA4s, inner Melbourne missing.*
3. **Nationally the question cannot be settled with the Census cost measure.** SDA sits in cheaper SA3s than approvals (-0.18, p 0.015; regression -25.59 (± 12.30)), but the 2021 median mortgage reads new housing as dear, so general building appears to lean *dear* (+0.13), which inflates the gap. On that measure pre-NDIS stock leans cheap as much as new SDA does. In Victoria the same measure gives approvals +0.35 where land prices give +0.02: the mortgage measure is the problem, not the finding.
4. **No sign that the factor's level draws building.** Across 409 pairs of adjacent SA3s either side of an SA4 border, the higher-factor side gets no more SDA per approval, and with the post-2023 factors less (+36.35 (± 31.73) per log point with pre-2023 factors, p 0.267; -42.30 (± 18.23) with post-2023 factors, p 0.036), nor did the July 2023 re-set move building (-38.30 (± 28.05), p 0.193). The lean does not steepen where cost spreads wider (+11.14 (± 18.00) per SD). This does not contradict point 2: factors largely track costs, so across a border the extra payment mostly buys dearer land.

**One-paragraph version:** New SDA is built where new housing is being built, concentrated in the growth corridors like other residential construction. In Victoria, where land prices can be measured directly, new SDA also leans toward the cheaper land within each region, beyond what general building, need or population explain, and older pre-NDIS stock does not — consistent with providers using a payment that is fixed across the SA4. There is no evidence that a higher factor, in itself, draws building across SA4 borders.

## What the data cannot carry

- **Victoria only for land cost.** NSW bulk land values are available only on request (requested; see *Open threads*). Queensland, South Australia and Western Australia publish no comparable free series.
- **SA3 publishes no building-type or category split of new build**, and a rise in enrolled HPS or Robust dwellings can include existing dwellings newly enrolled.
- **Lot prices, not prices per m².** VGV's vacant land is home sites under 4,000 m²; lots within that still vary in size.
- **Timing.** Building takes a year or more, so part of what was enrolled after the July 2023 factor re-set was committed before it.
- **Why, not just where.** Cheap land is also available land; providers also weigh participants, services, transport and hospitals. Need is controlled; the rest is not.

## The pricing record

`data/pricing/` holds every SDA Pricing Arrangements version from 2021-22 v1.0 to 2026-27 v1.0 (13 in all). New-build location factors changed only in 2023-24 v1.0 and 2023-24 v1.4: until 30 June 2023 one table served all stock; from 1 July 2023 every new build uses a separate new-build table, unchanged since its January 2024 revision.
